Areas of curiosity

Where my interests meet.

Technology gives me tools to build. Design and storytelling help me make the result meaningful.

01 / Artificial intelligence

Artificial intelligence

My current degree places artificial intelligence at the centre of my studies. I am interested in how intelligent systems can turn information into useful support for people, and how the design of those systems shapes the way people use them.

My experience in community service gives this interest a human perspective. I want to keep developing my technical understanding while thinking carefully about clarity, accessibility and the people affected by technology.

02 / Software development

Software development

My previous software engineering studies and current AI degree connect through an interest in building useful digital experiences. I enjoy the relationship between an idea, the structure behind it and the interface someone eventually uses.

This portfolio is a practical example: separate HTML pages, shared CSS and small JavaScript interactions come together to present information clearly. It gives me a place to keep developing and documenting my work.

03 / Interface design

UI/UX design

UI/UX design connects my interest in technology with communication. I am drawn to interfaces that make the next step understandable, with readable content, consistent navigation and clear feedback.

For this portfolio, that means thinking about the visitor’s experience across screen sizes and input methods. The same information should remain approachable whether someone uses a phone, a keyboard or a larger display.

04 / Visual storytelling

Cinematography & storytelling

My work as a cinematographer and editor at MNEX includes video and photo content, event coverage and advertising. I am interested in the way framing, pacing and editing help a story connect with its audience.

Visual storytelling also informs my interest in digital design. Choosing what to show, where to direct attention and how to organise a sequence matters in both a film and a website.
